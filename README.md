# ITS-AI-Intelligent-Transport-System
AI-Based Traffic Prediction, Analytics &amp; Route Optimization (Advanced Colab Notebook)

**AI-Based Traffic Prediction, Analytics & Route Optimization (Advanced Colab Notebook)**
**Pipeline:** DATA → ANALYSE → PREDICT → SEARCH → OPTIMIZE → DECIDE → VISUALIZE

**Dataset:** Smart Traffic Management Dataset (Kaggle) — 2,000 rows × 12 columns (timestamp, location ID, traffic volume, average speed, vehicle-type counts, weather, temperature, humidity, accident flag, signal status).

It is a data-driven traffic management system designed to help understand traffic conditions, predict congestion, estimate vehicle travel times, and identify better routes through a road network.

Traditional traffic management often relies on fixed traffic signals, manual observation, or simple historical averages. An AI-powered system can analyze several traffic-related variables together and use learned patterns to support better decisions.

**For example, imagine a city with five monitored road locations. The system receives or processes information such as:**
- Traffic volume and counts of cars, trucks, and bikes.
- Average vehicle speed.
- Weather, temperature, and humidity.
- Reported accidents and traffic signal status.
- Timestamp and road location.
Using this information, the project builds features, trains machine-learning models, evaluates their predictions, analyzes traffic patterns, and compares algorithms that can find routes through a road network.
The central objective

**The project attempts to answer six important questions:**

1. What is happening on the road now?
Analyze current or recorded traffic conditions and classify congestion.

2. What might happen next?
Forecast future traffic patterns and congestion levels.

3. How long will a journey take?
Estimate travel time and traffic-related measurements using regression.

4. Which route is preferable?
Compare graph-search algorithms and traffic-aware route costs.

5. What patterns exist in the traffic data?
Use clustering, exploratory data analysis, correlations, and statistical tests.

6. How can the findings support decisions?
Present predictions, comparisons, patterns, and evaluation results in reports and dashboards.



The key idea is that AI predicts and analyzes traffic conditions, while graph algorithms help search for routes. Both contribute to the larger traffic-management objective, but they are different technical components.
